# codex-sim: sim/
You own `sim/`. Pure TypeScript, no DOM, deterministic given a seed. This is the heart of gameplay: make it FUN, Soul Knight boss room feel.
- `kits.ts`: playerKitFromProfile — equal stat budget redistributed by repo shape. Weapon by primaryLanguage (e.g. Rust->cannon, C/C++->shotgun, Go->smg, TS/JS->blaster, Python/Jupyter->staff, Java/Kotlin/C#->laser, Ruby/PHP/Lua/others->blade; tweak). Skill by shape: high testRatio->hotfix, many cycles->rollback, very modular (low fanIn concentration)->firewall, many contributors->fork, else overclock. Title like "Rust Paladin". Palette from language color + seed. bossKitFromProfile: limbs from components (cycle members weakPoint), attacks generated from components (each limb sources 1–2 attacks; kind chosen by component traits: big loc->ring/spiral, high fanOut->fan, deep chain->laser, cycles->cascade, many files->summon, heart->slam/charge). Hacky attack names ("rm -rf /", "Merge Conflict", "Segfault Storm", "Dependency Hell", "npm install", "Stack Overflow", "git push --force", "Null Pointer", "Race Condition", "Memory Leak"...) plus component name. Theme by language/age. Boss name e.g. "FLASK, THE MICRO-HORROR".
- `match.ts`: authoritative step at fixed dt. Player movement with arena walls, dash (i-frames, cooldown), auto-aim to nearest boss limb/heart/minion within range when aim null, weapons per kind (energy cost, fire rate, spread, projectile speed), skills. Soul Knight armor: regenerates after 3s no damage; damage hits armor first. Downed -> teammate within 24px for 2s revives at 50% hp. All down -> defeat.
- Boss: hitbox regions = limbs (positions around the body, deterministic from seed; expose them in state? if renderer needs limb positions, add a contract request) + heart core. Limb damage also chips core HP; heart takes 1.5x, weakPoint limbs 1.5x. Sync combo: hits on same limb by different players within 0.5s => combo multiplier. Breaking limb disables its attacks. Phases: 2 at 50% (enrage, faster cooldowns), 3 at 15% (heart shield/last stand pattern).
- `bossAi.ts`: utility AI ~5 Hz: score attacks by threat (top DPS player), opportunity (low hp / not dashing / clustered players for ring/slam), cooldowns; always telegraph (>=0.6s) via casting + "telegraph" event; slow repositioning; charge aims at a player.
- Events into MatchState.events for every hit/telegraph/limb break/down/revive/skill/phase/end.
- `sim/bots.ts` + test: bot players (dodge nearest bullets, keep distance, fire, revive) — simulate 1/4/8 players vs every data/fallback boss (if codex-repo hasn't produced them yet, synthesize profiles), print win rate & duration table into docs/progress/codex-sim.md; tune to BRIEF balance targets.
